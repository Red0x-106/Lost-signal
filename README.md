# LOST SIGNAL

A small browser-based puzzle game made by **red0x**.

You receive strange signals, decode clues, and follow the trail to find out where they lead.
## LOST SIGNAL WEBSITE

->https://red0x-106.github.io/Lost-signal.github.io/

## What is it?

LOST SIGNAL is a multi-page puzzle website where each signal gives you something to figure out before you can move forward.

The puzzles aren't meant to be super complicated. They're more about noticing clues, trying things, and figuring out what the website is hiding.

## How it works

**Signal 01** starts with a strange number sequence:

18 - 5 - 4 - 0 - 24 

The numbers lead to the first answer and unlock the next part.

**Signal 02** changes things up. Instead of directly decoding another sequence, you have to use clues from the previous signal to interact with the page and discover what comes next.

The later clues eventually lead outside the puzzle itself.

## Why I made it

I wanted to make something that wasn't just another normal website.

The main idea was to make a small mystery/puzzle that someone could actually interact with instead of just reading.

A lot of the project was me experimenting, breaking things, fixing them, and changing the design until it felt right.
